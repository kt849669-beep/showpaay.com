Discover the ShowPay App

ShowPay brings account access, INR and USDT screens, payment tools and team activity into a mobile interface. This guide helps you recognise each screen and locate the options already available in the app.

Start with the login button above to open your account at showpayy.com. The web version works in your phone's browser, so you can view your account without installing a separate app.

The home screen groups your balance, received payments, top-up activity and team commission. It also displays a USDT conversion ratio and shortcuts to payment tools. Values in these screenshots are examples; your account shows your current information.

Get Started with ShowPay

New to the app? Use Create Account to reach the registration destination linked from the existing ShowPay login page. If you already have an account, choose Login Securely.

Creating your account

The registration screen collects your account details. Complete the fields shown by the registration service and review them before submitting.

  • Open registration. Select Create Account at the top of this page.
  • Enter your phone number. Use the number you want to associate with your account.
  • Choose a password. Keep your sign-in details private.
  • Check the invitation code. The existing registration link on showpayy.com uses the code below.
2invite5p6

Accessing an existing account

  • Open the login page using Login Securely.
  • Enter your details in the phone-number and password fields.
  • Need help signing in? Use the password-help option available on the login screen.

Your Profile & Account

Open the Me section to find profile information, account activity and settings in one place. This is also where you can locate the contact panel.

Find the setting you need

  • Profile details: View your account identifier and profile information.
  • Account history: Recharge History and Token History organise previous activity.
  • Bank Card: Locate the payment details associated with your account.
  • Language and security: Find language preferences and Google Authentication options.

Use the Top up shortcut to reach the deposit screen. For a question about your account, open the contact option inside the app.

Explore the Deposit Screens

The deposit area separates INR and USDT. Choose the relevant tab in your account to see the available options, current amounts and any applicable conditions.

The INR view

The INR screenshot contains order rows, filters and Buy controls. Income and Quota are shown alongside each order amount. Read the details in your account before selecting an order.

The USDT view

The USDT screen presents deposit information separately from INR. For your own deposit details, open your signed-in account; the image here is a visual example.

Find more information in the ShowPay USDT guide.

UPI & Payment Screens

Payment tools bring together the account and channel options shown by ShowPay. Open the relevant tool from your home screen to view the choices available to your account.

Adding a payment account

The illustrated setup screen organises the process into a sequence, with channel selection followed by phone and verification details. Follow the prompts in your own account.

Viewing payment activity

Use the payment screen to review the status of an order and the details associated with it. Your bank or UPI app provides the record of money actually received or sent.

Your Team Dashboard

The Team screen presents network activity and reward summaries. Its separate panels make it easier to distinguish commission, recharge activity and weekly totals.

Reading the team summary

  • Commission: View the figure recorded in your account.
  • Team Recharge: Find the recharge summary for your team.
  • Weekly Quota: A and A+B appear as separate totals.
  • Last Week: Compare the previous period using the screen's controls.

Finding your invitation

ShowPay Questions & Answers

Quick help with the pages and options covered in this guide.

Where do I sign in?
Use Login Securely to open showpayy.com, then enter your account details.
How do I open registration?
Select Create Account at the top. It opens the registration link already used by this website.
What if I forget my password?
Open the login page and use its password-help option to see the available recovery steps.
Where are the deposit options?
Open Top up or the deposit area in your account. INR and USDT are presented as separate tabs.
Are these my account balances?
No. The images illustrate the interface. Sign in to see the information associated with your own account.
Where can I find my team activity?
Open Team to view the commission, recharge and weekly quota panels recorded in your account.
How do I find account settings?
The Me section contains profile, history, bank-card, language and authentication options.
Where is the app download?
The app-access guide explains the browser version. This website does not currently host a direct APK installer.
How do I contact support?
Use the contact option inside your signed-in profile to see the support destinations provided there.

Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This notice describes the promotional website at showpayy.com. The linked Show Pay platform is a separate service with its own privacy practices.

Information on this website

This website has no sign-up form, login form, payment form or contact form. It does not ask for your name, phone number, passwords, OTPs or payment details.

The calculator and invite code

Calculator amounts are processed inside your browser. The website code does not send those amounts to a server or save them in cookies or local storage. Copying the invite code, when available, writes only that code to your device clipboard after you press Copy code.

Cookies and analytics

The supplied website does not include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, cookies or local-storage tracking. The hosting provider may process normal request data, such as an IP address, browser information, requested page and request time, to deliver the website and maintain security.

External app and Telegram links

Register and Download app buttons take you to app-web.showpay-web.com with a referral code. Open Show Pay and login buttons open showpayy.com. Any information you enter on that destination is handled by its operator. Review the destination’s policies before submitting information. Login and password-reset links open showpayy.com. Telegram links open the supplied showpayyoffical channel on t.me, which has its own privacy practices.

Updates and account questions

If this website later adds forms, analytics or other data collection, this notice should be updated to describe that change. For information held by your Show Pay account, use the platform’s own support and privacy options.
